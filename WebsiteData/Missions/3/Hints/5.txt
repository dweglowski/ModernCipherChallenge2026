What happens when you look at every 7th byte?
<code>
ciphertext = "0110101101100000101100111100000010111110100010111111011001010110" # ...
N = 7
nth_bytes = [[] for _ in range(N)] # Create N empty lists, one per offset

for i in range(0, len(ciphertext), 8):
    byte = ciphertext[i:i+8]
    
    offset = (i // 8) % N

    nth_bytes[offset].append(byte)

for offset in range(N):
    print(f"Offset {offset}:")
    unique = set(nth_bytes[offset])
    print(f"  Unique bytes: {len(unique)}")
</code>
Especially compared to other values of N, ~30 unique bytes is lower than we would expect.
Why could this be significant? Remember, you expect to see punctuation and some capital letters as well.
