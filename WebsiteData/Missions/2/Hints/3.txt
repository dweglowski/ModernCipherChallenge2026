Base64 takes in a blob of binary data and spits out a blob containing only 64 different printable ASCII characters.
Since 64 = 2^6, each character represents 6 bits of data.
In order from 0 (0b000000) to 63 (0b111111):
<code>
ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZabcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz0123456789+/
</code>
Try to convert the ciphertext back into a binary blob.
