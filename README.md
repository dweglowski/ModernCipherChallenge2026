# Modern Cipher Challenge 2026
Open for registrations. Competition starts October 5th.

## About Modern Cipher Challenge 2026

Modern Cipher Challenge 2026 is a student made and led cryptography competition inspired by the National Cipher Challenge. It will run in parallel with the NCC, providing students an opportunity to learn about modern cryptography through a NCC style challenges.

Across ten challenges, participants will progress through a series of increasingly difficult cipher-related problems, with each challenge introducing new techniques and concepts relevant to modern day cryptography.

## Who is MCC 2026 for?
This challenge is designed to challenge all participants. It primarily targets students from secondary school to university who enjoy National Cipher Challenge and want to take part in a fun side competition.

Most participants are expected and encouraged to use the hint system for each challenge, which, over several days of hints, will build up the knowledge to be able to solve each challenge themselves. More experienced students might also require several hints in the harder final challenges.

## When does it start?
The challenge starts on the 5th of October 2026 and runs through until the 10th of March 2027.
The first 3 missions are practice missions and not used for the total leaderboard.
